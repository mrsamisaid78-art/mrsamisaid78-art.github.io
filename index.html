What is Daily Money Guard?

Every month you say "this time I'll save" and then run out of money before the month ends? Daily Money Guard is an Android app that works out your safe daily spending limit from your balance, income and commitments.

  • Safe daily limit calculated automatically.
  • Log and edit expenses in seconds.
  • Track your income sources and monthly commitments.
  • Smart alerts when you get close to or pass your daily limit.
  • Track your savings.
  • Supports world currencies; works in Arabic and English.
